Job

Paste a Deezer playlist, YouTube playlist, or YouTube video URL.

1 · Create

Creates the job and starts it immediately, or queues it if another pipeline is already running. Queued jobs start automatically when the current one finishes. After an app restart, interrupted / queued jobs resume on their own.

2 · Recovery

Resume continues a cancelled / failed / incomplete job that still has pending work (including resolve with no tracks). Retry failures resets match/download errors then restarts — use that after a completed job with failed tracks.

Failures are listed below and logged to _work/<job-id>/failures.log.
